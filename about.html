ABOUT

Hi, I’m Claire Jacobs Gáez.

I’m a fintech product person by day and a nosy neighbor in San Francisco by night.

I’m interested in payments, commerce, and experiences that make social mobility and financial resilience achievable for working-class Americans. I graduated from the University of San Francisco in '21 with a BS in Entrepreneurship and have been working in venture capital and payments ever since.

My professional work spans: building a portfolio and a founder support function at an early-stage VC firm, customer research and product development for client dashboards at Onbe, and now at Lithic creating product usage feedback loops and notification campaigns to improve feature adoption.

I’m always excited to meet more kind, curious, and creative people and work on projects at the intersection of technology and local culture. Say hello.
